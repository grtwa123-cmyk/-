Notes
  • Impulse J = m·Δv depends only on the egg's mass and impact speed.
  • Average force F̄ = J / Δt. Longer collisions give smaller forces.
  • Peak force is what breaks the egg. For a half-sine, peak ≈ (π/2) × average.
