Formulas
  • Decay law: N(t) = N₀·2^(−t/T½) = N₀·e^(−λt)
  • Decay constant: λ = ln2 / T½
  • Activity: A = λ·N
  • Mean lifetime: τ = 1/λ = T½ / ln2
Notes
  • Each nucleus decays independently with a fixed probability per unit time — it has no age and no memory. The single-nucleus rule is pure chance.
  • Averaged over many nuclei that randomness becomes the smooth exponential: every half-life T½ the count halves, N₀ → N₀/2 → N₀/4 → …, regardless of how many you start with.
  • The live curve wanders around the theoretical one by roughly √N — shrink N₀ and the statistical scatter becomes obvious; raise it and the law tightens.
