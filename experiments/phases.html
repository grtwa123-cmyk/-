States of Matter — One Potential, Three Phases

Every particle here pulls weakly on its distant neighbours and shoves hard on its close ones. That single curve — the Lennard-Jones potential — is the whole model. Nothing in the code knows what a solid is, or a liquid, or a gas. Turn the temperature down and the particles settle into a lattice; turn it up and the lattice fails and they flow; turn it up further and they fly apart. Drop the density and they pull themselves into a droplet with vapour around it, because that is what the same force does when there is room.

  • Pressure: P = (N T* + ½Σr⋅f)/A

What to look for
  • There is no code for solid, liquid or gas. There is one potential and Newton's second law. Cold, the particles sit on a triangular lattice and the order parameter ψ₆ sits near 0.9; warm it past about T* = 0.4 and ψ₆ collapses while the diffusion constant leaves zero. That is melting happening, not melting being drawn.
  • The clearest evidence is the pair distribution g(r) beneath the box. A solid gives sharp spikes at the lattice shells. A liquid keeps one broad first peak — neighbours still touch, but the order is gone by the third shell. A gas is flat at 1: no structure at all beyond the hole each particle keeps around itself.
  • Nothing sets the speeds either. Every particle starts with exactly the same speed, pointed somewhere at random — a spike where Maxwell and Boltzmann predict a spread. Collisions alone redistribute it, until the histogram lands on the curve drawn over it. That is also where the temperature reading comes from, since T* is just the mean kinetic energy.
  • Drop the density to 0.12 and cool it. The particles do not spread out to fill the box. Droplets nucleate — two or three of them, wherever the crowding happened to be — each with a sharp surface and a thin vapour around it, and then they drift and merge, slowly, until one is left. Nothing put a surface there and nothing scheduled the merge. Heat the same system and it boils away again. Turn the thermostat off at any point and the total energy is conserved to a tenth of a percent, so none of this is being driven.
