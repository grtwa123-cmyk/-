Predator & Prey

Foxes eat rabbits; rabbits breed; foxes starve when rabbits run out. Two simple rules — the Lotka–Volterra equations — make the two populations chase each other in endless cycles, the predators always lagging a quarter-step behind their prey.

Formulas
  • Prey: dx/dt = α·x − β·x·y
  • Predator: dy/dt = δ·x·y − γ·y
  • Fixed point: x* = γ/δ, y* = α/β
  • Conserved: V = δx − γ ln x + βy − α ln y
Notes
  • Neither species settles down: the populations orbit the fixed point forever, and the predator peak always trails the prey peak by a quarter cycle.
  • The phase portrait (right) traces one closed loop — the model conserves the quantity V exactly, so the orbit never spirals in or out.
  • Raising the prey growth α or lowering the predator death γ shifts the equilibrium and stretches the cycle; the readout V should stay essentially constant, a check on the integrator.
